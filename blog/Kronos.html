The Problem: Financial data suffers from high noise and non-stationary patterns that general-purpose time-series foundation models struggle to capture. A decoder-only Transformer is then pre-trained on these tokens, enabling unified support for forecasting, classification, and anomaly detection tasks. The live demo is hosted at the Kronos demo page; locally, the WebUI serves interactive K-line forecasting and result export.
